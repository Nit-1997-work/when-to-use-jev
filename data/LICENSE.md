# Data license

The files in `data/splits/` and the result records published under `results/` contain text derived from the Bitext Retail (eCommerce) Tagged Training Dataset for LLM-based Virtual Assistants (Bitext, 2024).
That dataset is licensed under the Community Data License Agreement - Sharing, Version 1.0 (CDLA-Sharing-1.0), and these derived files are shared under the same license: <https://cdla.dev/sharing-1-0/>

The source, pinned revision, and checksum are in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md), and the dataset card is in [raw/README.md](raw/README.md).
